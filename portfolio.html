Problem Statement

Raw data is slow to turn into answers

Analysts lose hours translating raw files into executive summaries and SQL queries — and general-purpose AI tools can't be trusted with the numbers.

⏳

Hours of manual work

Analysts spend hours turning raw CSV files into executive summaries and SQL queries by hand.

⚠

LLMs hallucinate

General-purpose LLMs invent figures and cannot perform exact computation on a dataset.

💬

Prompting is a skill

Non-technical teams struggle to construct a robust prompt that gets a reliable answer.

💻

SQL is a barrier

Stakeholders need answers fast, but writing correct SQL for every question takes time and expertise.

Performance

A fast open-source model on Groq targets insights within 3 seconds, and Google Sheets are cached so they aren't re-downloaded on every interaction.

Accuracy by Design

Every number on screen comes from Pandas, not the LLM — removing the hallucination risk for metrics.

Privacy

Only column names, data types and the question are sent to the AI. The dataset's rows never leave the app.
